Shift Based Driver Hours of Service Model

Analytics Engineering

Shift Based Driver Hours of Service Model

Need

Develop a reliable analytical model that accurately represented driver Hours of Service activity across operational shifts, supporting compliance reporting and transportation analytics.

Solution

Designed a shift-based data model that transformed raw telematics events into complete operational timelines, accounting for shift boundaries, time zones, duty status transitions, and daily reporting requirements.

Challenge

Driver activity rarely aligned with calendar days. Building an accurate model required resolving complex edge cases including overnight shifts, timezone differences, gaps in activity, and multiple reporting perspectives while maintaining consistent business logic.

Result

Delivered a robust analytical model that provided a reliable foundation for compliance reporting and operational analysis, transforming complex event data into trusted, shift-oriented metrics for downstream reporting.
